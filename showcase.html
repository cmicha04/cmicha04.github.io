MICROSURGERY SIMULATOR

What this simulator can do

Clipping of a right IC-PC (internal carotid–posterior communicating artery) aneurysm through a pterional, transsylvian approach, in the browser. Each item links straight to it.

For education and demonstration only. This is a simplified model. It is not a clinical training tool, it is not validated for skill assessment, and nothing in it is medical advice.

The case

  • The full operationOpen the sim
    Six stages: open the sylvian fissure, identify M1, the ICA and optic nerve, confirm the PCom, dissect the neck, clip, and check the PCom stays open. A checklist and a mentor guide each step; press ? for controls.
  • Original version (M6, before the real brain)./original/
    The simulator frozen as of September 28: the complete case with the simple procedural lobes and the first microscope view, before any work on the real brain model. (Only the Hide panels button was added later.)
  • Demo modeOpen, then ▶ DEMO
    The sim plays the case for you with the same tools you use. It keeps playing while you touch the screen; press ■ STOP DEMO to take over.
  • DebriefOpen, then END CASE
    Clip result (closure, residual neck, ICA narrowing, branch occlusion), time, blood loss and tips.

Surgical tools and checks

  • Ten instrumentsKeys 1–0
    Suction, micro scissors, bipolar, dissector, brain retractor, aneurysm clip (straight or curved), ICG, micro Doppler, endoscope, temporary clip.
  • Bleeding and vital signsOpen the sim
    Oozing and rupture, blood pooling, suction, a live ECG and arterial pressure trace, and motor evoked potentials that react to blood flow.
  • Checking the clipKeys 7, 8, 9
    ICG video angiography, micro Doppler sound, and an endoscope view behind the neck show whether the aneurysm is closed and the PCom still fills.

Anatomy and look

  • Real brain modelDefault
    An MRI-based right hemisphere, split into frontal and temporal lobes along the sylvian fissure, with pink pial surface vessels drawn in the shader.
  • Today's sim with the simple lobes?cortex=procedural
    The current version with everything added since, but the procedural lobes in place of the real brain. (For the untouched September 28 version, see Original version above.)
  • Anatomy at real depthPREVIEW?view=m3
    Work in progress: the surgeon's view from right pterional operative videos (frontal lobe left, temporal lobe right), the carotid cistern about 45 mm below the fissure opening, and the vessels and nerves at real sizes and spacing, placed in the opening the retracted lobes leave. The demo (▶ DEMO) plays the whole case here.
  • M3 turned to the video orientationPREVIEW?view=m3&roll=201
    The same preview turned so the frontal lobe is on the left, the temporal lobe on the right and the fissure vertical, as in the right pterional reference videos. Compare with the default 70° roll; ?roll= takes any angle.
  • Rigid retraction (before soft)?soft=0
    Soft retraction is now the default: the lobes bend under the retractor blade (cut the sylvian arachnoid with the scissors (2), then use the retractor (5)). This switches back to the old version, where each lobe slides as one block.
  • Tissue look panel?tweak=1
    Live sliders and colour pickers for every tissue and for the surface vessels; copy the values as config.

Devices

  • Quality tiers?quality=low ?quality=high
    Picked automatically for your device and adjusted to hold the frame rate; LOW runs on integrated graphics and phones. Also in the top bar.
  • Touch layout?touch=1
    Phones and tablets: pinch to zoom, two fingers to pan, a View / Tool switch for one finger, and Hide panels (top left, or H on a keyboard) to fold every panel away and see the anatomy. Forced on a desktop with this link.
